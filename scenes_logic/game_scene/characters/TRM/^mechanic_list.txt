Airborne Guard
    18 frames blockstun when landling after block in the air.
    Landing while in air blockstun will replace any remaining air blockstun with ground blockstun.

Landing Recovery
    Whenever the player lands from being in the air,they experience landing recovery.
    During the first 5 frames of landing the following actions are disabled: 
        - forward/backward walk,all jumps,and backdash
    All other actions are available immediately on landing 
        - notably attacking,guarding,run,and forward dash

Disabling Blue Burst
    Bursts will be disabled while:
    Being thrown
    Being hit by a hitgrab
    Being hit by an Overdrive
    Being hit by attacks that force the opponent into non-standard hitstun,
    such as a Wall Stick state
